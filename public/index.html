How it works

Set your hoursMark the times you are open each week, once or repeating. Booked time goes in beside it, in any color you like.
Share your linkYour calendar lives at {host}/you. Send the link to anyone - a parent, a student, a client. They need no account.
Get requests by emailA visitor picks an open time and leaves their name, email and phone. You get an email, and you accept or decline in one click.
